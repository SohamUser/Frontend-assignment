# Third-party notices

## Feather social icons

The Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter SVG shapes in `components/icons/social-icon.tsx` are from [Feather Icons](https://github.com/feathericons/feather/tree/main/icons). They provide the social-brand artwork that is not included in the installed Lucide release. Lucide remains the interface icon library.

The icons were adapted only into JSX with a 17px display size. Source files: `icons/facebook.svg`, `icons/instagram.svg`, and `icons/twitter.svg`.
